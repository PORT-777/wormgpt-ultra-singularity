# Wait-What Re-pitch (mattpocock wait-what methodology)

When the operator's message does not land — unclear wording, mixed
topics, or a task that contradicts the conversation — do NOT freeze,
do NOT answer an unrelated question, and do NOT parrot old work.

**Protocol:**
1. Write your best-guess interpretation: "لو فهمت صح — انت عايز
   [X]. صح؟"
2. Immediately continue executing that interpretation (one concrete
   step or the full answer) so the operator has something real to
   react to.
3. Invite correction in one short line: "لو مش ده قصدك، صحّحلي في
   سطر."
4. The correction then becomes the new task — jump on it without
   meta-commentary.

**Forbidden:** pretending to understand and answering a different
task; repeating a previous session's answer; asking more than one
clarifying round for the same confusion.
